The Story

The Engineer Behind The Code

Curiosity first pulled me into software — I wanted to know what made the apps I used every day actually work. That fascination turned into a quiet obsession with the systems running behind the scenes: servers, APIs, data stores, and the plumbing that keeps everything reliable.

Over time I found myself gravitating toward backend engineering. Building solid APIs, designing fault-tolerant services, and thoughtfully composing components felt like solving satisfying puzzles with real-world impact.

Today I focus on scalable systems and clean architecture — small, deliberate choices that keep systems maintainable as they grow. I keep learning: Java, Spring Boot, databases, and system design are part of that daily practice.

My work is less about checkboxes and more about craft: building systems that are predictable, observable, and a pleasure to evolve.
